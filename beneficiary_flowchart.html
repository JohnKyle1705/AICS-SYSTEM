Beneficiary Operational Lifecycle Specifications

Stage Step Name Role System Rule & Validation Standard Database Table Resulting State
Stage 1 Resident Registration & OTP Resident Password strength validation (8+ chars, upper, lower, number, special). Requires 6-digit OTP confirmation. profiles, auth.users is_active = false
Stage 2 Admin Residency Gating MSWDO Admin Admin verifies residency in Barangay Pajo before activating account. Unverified users wait at /pending. profiles is_active = true
Stage 3 Beneficiary Profiling Gate Resident Mandatory demographic registry (Purok, civil status, 11-digit phone 09XXXXXXXXX, PWD/Senior/4Ps, family size). New Request is locked until completed. beneficiaries Beneficiary Record Linked
Stage 4 Anti-Duplication Guard System Engine Prevents multiple active requests (Pending/Approved) and enforces the 30-day municipal cooling-off restriction. requests, system_settings Passed / Blocked
Stage 5 Category Details & Docs Resident Medical: Hospital, diagnosis, cost, medical certificate & hospital bill.
Burial: Deceased name, relationship, funeral home (Bogo to Daanbantayan), death cert & contract.		 requests, assistance_requirements Form Draft Completed
Stage 6 Valid ID Photos & Submit Resident Front Side & Back Side photos of government-issued ID required (max 10 MB). Stored securely in Supabase storage bucket documents. requests, request_documents status = Pending
Stage 7 MSWDO Social Assessment Social Worker / Admin Social worker evaluates crisis documents, conducts intake interview, and issues decision. Approve specifies approved grant amount in PHP. assessments, requests Approved or Declined
Stage 8 Treasury Voucher Prep Municipal Treasurer Treasurer validates municipal cash reserves, prepares Disbursement Voucher (DV), and marks readiness. payouts, funds Ready for Release
Stage 9 Cash Payout & Ledger Release Beneficiary / Treasurer Beneficiary presents original Valid ID at Municipal Treasury, signs disbursement payroll sheet. Treasurer confirms atomic payout in ledger. payouts, fund_transactions status = Released
